OBJECTIVE

You are a UAP (Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon) being hunted by military forces. Survive as long as possible against endless waves of enemies. Destroy hostiles to collect salvage and level up your weapons.

CONTROLS

WASD Move
SPACE Dash (3 charges)
Q Overdrive
E Shield
F Chrono Burst
R Ultimate
ESC Pause

GAMEPLAY

  • Weapons fire automatically - focus on positioning and dodging
  • Dash (SPACE) is always available with 3 energy charges that regenerate
  • Power-ups (Q/E/F/R) drop from enemies and are one-time use
  • Green salvage orbs give XP - level up to choose new weapons or upgrades
  • 5 Phases escalate difficulty: Detected → Engaged → Reinforced → Scramble → Containment

POWER-UPS

  • ⚡ Overdrive (Q) - 3x fire rate, 2x damage for 8 seconds
  • 🛡️ Shield (E) - Invulnerable for 6 seconds, damages nearby enemies
  • ⏱️ Chrono Burst (F) - Slow enemies, speed up, magnetize XP for 8 seconds
  • ⭐ Ultimate (R) - Your chosen ultimate ability (select before run)

FAQ

  • How do I shoot? Weapons fire automatically at nearby enemies.
  • Why can't I use Q/E/F/R? These must drop from enemies first - they're one-time use items.
  • What are the glowing icons? Power-up drops. Walk over them to collect.
  • How do I unlock new craft? Visit the HANGAR from the main menu.
  • What is Resonance? Currency earned each run. Spend it on permanent UPGRADES.
  • What does the Ultimate do? Choose your Ultimate before starting - Droplet makes you invincible and ram enemies, Replicators spawn attack drones.
  • How do I survive longer? Keep moving, use Dash to dodge, prioritize weapon upgrades that match your playstyle.
